Exterior Window Cleaning at Shark Exterior

At Shark Exterior, we specialize in Exterior Window Cleaning, ensuring your windows shine with crystal-clear perfection every time. Our expert service not only enhances the outside appearance of your property but also prolongs the lifespan of your windows by preventing dirt and grime buildup that can etch into the glass over time.

We use a pure water-fed pole system combined with our proprietary Shark RainBlock Technology — a hydrophobic coating applied after every clean that causes water to bead and roll off instead of forming streaks. That means your windows stay cleaner, longer, and are protected between every visit.

To complement our meticulous cleaning process, Shark Exterior offers flexible recurring window cleaning services. These memberships are designed to keep your windows consistently pristine without the hassle of scheduling regular appointments. Our clients enjoy sparkling windows all year round.

FAQs About Exterior Window Cleaning

Everything you need to know about exterior window cleaning

01

How often should I have my exterior windows cleaned?

Most homeowners benefit from exterior window cleaning 2–4 times per year. Our Quarterly plan is the most popular — it aligns with Nebraska's seasonal changes and keeps your home looking great year-round. Monthly is ideal for those who want pristine windows at all times.

02

Do you use eco-friendly cleaning products?

Yes! We use biodegradable, eco-friendly cleaning solutions that are safe for your family, pets, and landscaping. Our pure water-fed pole system uses purified water that leaves zero residue and no harmful chemicals.

03

What happens if it rains after my cleaning?

That's what our 7-Day Rain Guarantee is for! If it rains within 7 days of your clean and leaves spots on your windows, we'll come back and re-clean them at absolutely no charge. Our Shark RainBlock coating also helps water bead off rather than leaving streaks.

04

Do I need to be home during the exterior cleaning?

Nope! For exterior-only cleaning, you don't need to be present. We'll notify you when we arrive and when we're done, and we'll send before & after photos directly to your phone or email.

05

How long does an exterior window cleaning typically take?

Most residential exterior window cleans take 1–3 hours depending on your home's size and number of windows. Two-story homes and larger properties take a bit longer. We'll give you a time estimate when we confirm your appointment.

06

What is Shark RainBlock Technology?

Shark RainBlock is our proprietary hydrophobic coating applied to your windows after every clean. It causes water to bead and roll off rather than forming streaks, keeping your windows cleaner up to 3x longer between visits.

07

What areas do you serve?

Shark Exterior proudly serves Lincoln, Nebraska and Kearney, Nebraska, including surrounding communities in both areas. Not sure if we serve your address? Give us a call at (402) 309-0128 and we'll let you know right away.
