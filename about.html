Who We Are

Our Story

Shark Exterior started with a simple idea: exterior cleaning should be easy to book, done right the first time and backed by people who actually care about your home. We're a locally owned crew serving Lincoln and Kearney, Nebraska with exterior and interior window cleaning, house soft washing, power washing, gutter cleaning, solar panel cleaning and professional lighting installation.

Every job gets the same treatment — careful prep, pro-grade equipment, eco-friendly solutions and a final walk-through before we leave. It's why so many of our customers stay with us on a membership plan year after year.
