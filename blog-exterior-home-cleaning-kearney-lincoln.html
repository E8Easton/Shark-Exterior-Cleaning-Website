Exterior Home Cleaning in Lincoln and Kearney, NE: The Complete Guide

April 2025 8 min read Lincoln & Kearney, NE

Your home's exterior takes a beating in Nebraska. Between the Great Plains wind that pushes dust and pollen onto every surface, the hot dry summers, the humid growing season that feeds mold and algae, and the harsh winters that leave salt and mineral deposits everywhere — keeping your home's exterior clean isn't optional, it's maintenance. This guide covers everything Lincoln and Kearney homeowners need to know about exterior home cleaning, from windows to siding to gutters to driveways, and when to call a professional versus doing it yourself.

The Four Pillars of Exterior Home Cleaning in Nebraska

1. Window Cleaning

Clean windows have an outsized impact on how your home looks and feels. Dirty windows make even a well-maintained home look dull and neglected — and they reduce natural light inside. For Lincoln and Kearney homes, professional exterior window cleaning should happen at minimum twice a year, with quarterly cleaning giving the best ongoing results.

Nebraska's hard water is the main challenge for DIY window cleaning — tap water leaves mineral spots that make windows look worse after washing. Professional crews use purified, deionized water that dries completely clear.

2. Siding and House Washing

Your siding — whether vinyl, fiber cement, wood, or stucco — accumulates mold, mildew, algae, and road dust every year. In Nebraska, the humid summers make biological growth on north-facing and shaded siding especially common. The right method is soft washing: low-pressure application of biodegradable cleaning solutions that kill organic growth at the root. Results last 2 to 5 years depending on exposure and tree cover.

Never pressure wash vinyl siding. High pressure forces water behind panels, creating hidden mold problems inside your walls that are expensive and difficult to remediate.

3. Gutter Cleaning

Gutters are the most commonly neglected exterior maintenance item on Nebraska homes. With the cottonwood seeds, elm leaves, and seed pods that fall in Lincoln and Kearney, gutters can fill completely in a single fall season. Clogged gutters cause foundation damage, fascia rot, and ice dam formation in winter — all of which cost far more to fix than regular gutter cleaning.

Clean gutters twice per year: once in spring after cottonwood season ends, and once in fall after leaves drop. If you have heavy tree cover directly over your roof, add a midsummer check.

4. Driveway and Hard Surface Cleaning

Driveways, sidewalks, and patios accumulate years of tire marks, oil stains, biological growth, and road salt residue. Professional pressure washing removes all of it and dramatically improves curb appeal. For Lincoln and Kearney homeowners, spring is the ideal time — removing winter salt and sand before it works deeper into concrete pores.

The Nebraska Exterior Cleaning Calendar

Here's a simple seasonal schedule that covers all your exterior cleaning needs for a Kearney or Lincoln home:

  • Spring (April–June): Window cleaning after pollen season, soft wash house washing, driveway pressure washing to remove winter salt
  • Summer (July–August): Window cleaning mid-season if needed, solar panel cleaning before peak sun hours
  • Fall (September–November): Gutter cleaning after leaf drop, window cleaning before winter, Christmas light installation booking and installation
  • Winter: Window cleaning after any mud or salt events, gutter inspection after ice storms

The curb appeal formula for Lincoln and Kearney homes: Clean windows + freshly washed siding + clear gutters + pressure-washed driveway = a home that looks like it's worth 10% more than it did last week. It's the highest-ROI home improvement you can do without picking up a hammer.
