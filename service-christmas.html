House Lighting Installation at Shark Exterior

There's nothing quite like a home lit up beautifully for the holidays — but the installation process can be dangerous, time-consuming, and frustrating. At Shark Exterior, we handle the entire process for you: professional design, safe installation, and a prompt takedown after the season ends.

We use high-quality, energy-efficient LED lights and professional-grade clips and fasteners that won't damage your roof, gutters, or siding. Our team works safely at height with proper equipment and installs every strand evenly, cleanly, and securely — so your display looks polished and lasts through the entire holiday season.

We serve homeowners and businesses across Lincoln and Kearney, Nebraska. Whether you want a classic roofline display or a full landscape design with trees and bushes, we'll create a holiday look that impresses every visitor who drives by.

Why Choose Shark Exterior?

  • Professional LED Lights We use high-quality, energy-efficient LED strands that are bright, long-lasting, and weather-resistant.
  • Safe Roof & Gutter Installation Proper clips and fasteners that won't damage your roof, gutters, or siding — ever.
  • Takedown Service Included We come back after the holidays and take everything down cleanly and store lights for next year.
  • Custom Designs From simple rooflines to full landscape displays — we'll design a look that fits your home and your vision.

FAQ

FAQs About House Lighting Installation

Everything you need to know about Christmas light installation

01

Do you supply the lights or do I?

We can do either. We offer high-quality LED light packages that we provide, install, and store for you. Alternatively, we're happy to install your own lights if you prefer. Let us know your preference when booking.

02

When should I schedule installation?

We recommend booking your Christmas light installation in October or early November before the schedule fills up. Installation typically happens from mid-November onward so lights are ready well before the holiday season.

03

Is takedown included in the price?

Yes — takedown service is included with every Christmas light installation. We'll schedule a return visit after the holidays to remove everything cleanly and store your lights properly for next year.

04

Can you do commercial holiday lighting too?

Absolutely. We install holiday lighting for businesses, storefronts, offices, and commercial properties of all sizes. Contact us for custom commercial holiday lighting quotes.

05

What areas do you serve?

Shark Exterior proudly serves Lincoln, Nebraska and Kearney, Nebraska, including surrounding communities. Call us at (402) 309-0128 to book your holiday installation.
